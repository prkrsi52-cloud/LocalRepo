Web | Staging | Account Settings Page - Required fields are displayed in red even when the data is filled on "Are you sure you want to cancel" modal

Launch the URL "staging.marvel.com"
Login with valid MU credentials
Navigate to Marvel Subscription on Account Settings Page
Click on "Cancel Your Marvel Unlimited Subscription" hyperlink
Navigate to "Are you sure you want to cancel" modal
Click on "Cancel" button without filling any of the Required fields
Observe that all the required fields are displayed in red
Fill all the required fields and observe that required fields are still displayed in red.

Expected Result: Required fields should not be displayed in red when the required fields are filled on "Are you sure you want to cancel"
Actual Result: Required fields are displayed in red even when the data is filled on "Are you sure you want to cancel"
